# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leads, collaborators, and clients reviewing Marcus Nguyen's software engineering work. They are scanning for craft, technical judgment, credible shipped projects, and a clear way to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Marcus Nguyen as a software engineer who builds polished web products, full-stack systems, and thoughtful interfaces. Success means visitors quickly understand the work, trust the execution quality, and can move into projects, writing, or contact.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and quietly technical. The voice should feel direct and capable, with enough visual craft to prove taste without burying the content.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS gradients, repeated identical cards, heavy glass effects, decorative section numbering, fake dashboards, and copy that sounds inflated. The new preview should follow the supplied dark editorial portfolio reference while preserving Marcus's real content.

## Design Principles

Show the work before explaining it.

Make scanning effortless for hiring and collaboration contexts.

Use dark restraint with one consistent accent.

Let real project images, logos, and concise copy carry credibility.

Keep the route safe to preview without changing the live homepage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, semantic content structure, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support for any animation.
